================================================================================
METHODOLOGICAL NOTES
================================================================================

1. The number of replications is computed using the formula:
   r = (t_{α/2} × s / E)²
   where:
   - t_{α/2} is the critical value of the t distribution
   - s is the sample standard deviation
   - E is the desired precision (in absolute units)

2. The relative precision (E%) is converted to absolute: E = μ × (E% / 100)

3. The t distribution is used because the standard deviation is estimated from the sample.

4. The computation is iterative when needed for convergence.
